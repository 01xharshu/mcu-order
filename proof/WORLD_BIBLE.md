# World bible

**Status:** active, source-led baseline — 2026-07-24

## Product rule

The Continuum treats a film, a life, or a branch as a readable causal relationship rather than a database record. Graphite is quiet space; temporal film is a specific moment; an optical seam registers the relationship; constrained red marks an active cause.

## Route modes

| Route | Task | World behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/continuum` | understand the journey | aperture → temporal strata → branch |
| `/films` | find a changed event | focused release index and causal reading |
| `/characters` | enter a life | life record and relationship pressure |
| `/timeline` | compare two orders | registered release/chronology ribbon |
| `/watch` | choose a path | sequence reweave |
| `/sources` | inspect evidence | bright evidence plane |

## Canon policy

Released work and official Marvel information are the current primary source layer. Upcoming titles have `chronologicalOrder: null`, no full consequence, and an `unknown` confidence state. Unknown information is shown as unknown rather than filled with plausible copy.
