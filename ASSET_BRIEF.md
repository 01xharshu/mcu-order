# ASSET BRIEF — The MCU Chronicle v8

## Global Requirements

Every asset generated for the MCU Chronicle must adhere to the `Archival Graphic Realism` style defined in `ART_DIRECTION.md`.

## Tableau Generation Briefs

### 1. Origin (Iron Man)
- **Subject**: Tony Stark in an early Iron Man suit variation (Mark I or Mark III hybrid), viewed from behind or in partial silhouette, working on a workbench. Glowing arc reactor clearly visible.
- **Background**: Dark, cavernous workshop or cave interior. Warm, localized forge lighting.
- **Foreground**: Blueprints, mechanical tools, sparks in sharp focus.
- **Graphic Frame**: A literal or implied rectangular window cutting out the center.

### 2. Assemble (Avengers)
- **Subject**: Six distinct figures at staggered depths. No direct actor likenesses. Massing should suggest the original six (soldier, armor, god, spy, archer, brute).
- **Background**: War-torn urban environment, dust, rubble. Cool daylight.
- **Foreground**: Debris, concrete rebar breaking the frame.
- **Graphic Frame**: Diagonal fracture lines.

### 3. Worlds (Cosmic/Mythic/Earth)
- **Subject**: Triptych. Left: Mythic realm (gold/rainbow). Center: Earth (blue/gray). Right: Cosmic (purple/black).
- **Background**: Deep space and celestial bodies.
- **Foreground**: Energy trails connecting the three realms.
- **Graphic Frame**: Three vertical pillars.

### 4. Fracture (Civil War)
- **Subject**: Two opposing groups split by a physical or architectural void.
- **Background**: Airport tarmac or concrete bunker, gray and desaturated.
- **Foreground**: Shadows stretching away from each other.
- **Graphic Frame**: A hard vertical split.

### 5. Infinity (The Snap)
- **Subject**: An empty space dominating the frame. Remaining figures huddled or kneeling below.
- **Background**: Ash, atmospheric dust, warm fading sunlight (Wakanda).
- **Foreground**: Dissolving matter.
- **Graphic Frame**: A void that cuts into the content.

### 6. Legacy (New Era)
- **Subject**: Successor figures (shield bearer, archer, etc.) looking toward a light source.
- **Background**: Dawn light over a quiet landscape.
- **Foreground**: Silhouettes of the old generation's symbols (broken shield, empty helmet) out of focus.
- **Graphic Frame**: Ascending steps or horizon line.

### 7. Multiverse (Paths)
- **Subject**: Five distinct spatial routes (energy ribbons or pathways) diverging from a central nexus.
- **Background**: Infinite fractal space.
- **Foreground**: Abstract geometric shapes.
- **Graphic Frame**: A central circle or portal.

## Character Identity Sheets

For each flagship character, we need a 4-panel identity sheet:
1. **Silhouette/Massing**: Pure shape recognition.
2. **Material/Texture**: Close-up of key material (armor, fabric, skin).
3. **Lighting/Mood**: The character in their defining lighting environment.
4. **Symbol/Prop**: Close-up of their defining artifact (reactor, shield).
