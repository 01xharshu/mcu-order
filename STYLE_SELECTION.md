# STYLE SELECTION — The MCU Chronicle v8

## Hypothesis 1: Composition-Led (Graphic Design Focus)
- **Concept**: Hard-edge geometry, extreme minimalism, relying heavily on typography and negative space.
- **Rejection Reason**: While elegant, it strips the emotional weight from the MCU. The characters become abstract shapes rather than dramatic agents. Fails the franchise-swap test (could easily be a site for a high-end fashion brand or tech product).

## Hypothesis 2: Typography-Led (Editorial Focus)
- **Concept**: Massive display type, overlapping text layers, brutalist layout, with imagery treated as secondary texture.
- **Rejection Reason**: Overwhelms the narrative. The MCU is fundamentally about characters and visual spectacle, not typographic gymnastics. It makes the reading experience difficult and obscures the causal connections.

## Hypothesis 3: Spatial/Tableau-Led (Archival Graphic Realism) — SELECTED
- **Concept**: A 2.5D planar approach where highly detailed, moody graphic novel illustrations exist within strict editorial framing. The space inside the frame has depth (parallax), while the frame itself asserts the 2D surface of the screen.
- **Approval Reason**: Balances cinematic scale with archival rigor. The holding/revealing of layers works perfectly with the spatial separation of Foreground, Subject, and Background. It feels like an artifact belonging to the universe.

## Style Anchor

The selected style is `Archival Graphic Realism`.

**Core attributes:**
- Precise ink and watercolor texture.
- Muted, dramatic color palette.
- Moody atmospheric lighting.
- Strong graphic silhouettes.
- Complete absence of photorealistic actor likenesses (to maintain artistic distance and avoid likeness rights issues).
- No generic comic book "pop" or 3D plastic gloss.
