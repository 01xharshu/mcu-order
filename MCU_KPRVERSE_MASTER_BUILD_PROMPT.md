## 4. Chosen design direction

### Direction name

**The Continuum Engine**

This direction is locked. The coding agent must not replace it with a personal preference, trend, template, or averaged hybrid. Any comparison directions created during the process exist only to prove why this direction is stronger.

### Design statement

> The visitor enters one impossible, evolving model of MCU continuity. At first it is almost empty: a dark field, one cut of light, one monumental line of type. With every decision, the field gains depth. Lives become translucent time-slices; relationships become tension; events fold the world; loss removes physical volume; legacy reweaves it; the multiverse turns one line into incompatible paths. The experience is not a depiction of advanced technology. It is an advanced cinematic instrument whose behavior makes an immense story understandable.

### Emotional promise

The visitor should feel that a familiar film universe has become physically legible around them. The emotional sequence is:

```text
anticipation → awe → agency → convergence → fracture → loss → inheritance → uncertainty → personal choice
```

The experience starts restrained enough to feel adult, grows spectacular only when the story earns it, and ends in clarity rather than visual exhaustion.

### Primary visual tension

Calm, intelligent editorial control versus impossible transformations of time, identity, and consequence.

### Dominant compositional rule

Every stable viewport contains:

1. one dominant human, event, typographic, or temporal mass;
2. one primary editorial finding;
3. at least `28%` uninterrupted negative space on desktop and `18%` on mobile;
4. one continuity seam explaining the relationship between states;
5. one motivated light event;
6. no more than one active effect family.

The dominant mass may sit partly outside the viewport. Symmetry is reserved for convergence, fracture, and explicit comparison. Do not center every hero.

### Signature matter

The world is made from three classes of story matter:

1. **Light-absorbing graphite space** — the quiet base and source of negative space.
2. **Luminous temporal film** — thin, translucent image/type strata carrying a specific moment, person, or claim.
3. **Causal seams** — hairline registrations where two states meet; neutral white until a selected cause receives a restricted ion-red edge.

These are not UI materials, panels, holograms, screens, or architecture. They are the visual physics of the narrative.

### Signature interaction

**Causal Shear**: the visitor holds and drags a real seam through a registered scene. The scene separates into `BEFORE`, `CHOICE`, and `AFTER` strata at different depths and velocities. Pull distance maps to causal distance, not effect intensity. Resistance rises as the consequence widens. Exact relationships appear inside the created gap. Release causes the layers to re-register and reweave.

This is the one interaction the website is allowed to be known for.

It is not a portal, scanner, lens, circular reveal, cursor distortion, slider comparison, generic hold effect, or KPR imitation. Its meaning must remain understandable in a still capture.

## 5A. The Continuum Engine world bible

This section governs the whole website and replaces literal environment world-building.

### World premise

The Continuum Engine is not an in-universe place, fictional company, device, operating system, or piece of Avengers technology. It is the visual form of the visitor’s understanding. The world begins as almost nothing and acquires structure as the visitor learns.

The public product name remains:

```text
THE MCU CONTINUUM
```

`Continuum Engine` is a production concept, not a label to repeat in headings.

### Behavioral physics

The world has seven non-negotiable verbs:

| Narrative idea | Spatial behavior | Visual evidence | Never represent it as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | one razor-thin aperture introduces depth into a flat field | light and image occupy a slit before widening | boot screen, door, portal |
| Relationship | two time-slices bend toward one another and share tension | their distance and angle respond together | node graph, connecting neon line |
| Convergence | independent strata synchronize into one dominant plane | separate velocities resolve at the same beat | hero lineup, logo reveal |
| Fracture | one registered world shears into incompatible but related planes | shared imagery splits while correspondence remains visible | shattered glass, glitch |
| Loss | a previously occupied volume is truly absent | background, type, light, and audio refuse to fill it | dust particles, fade-to-black |
| Inheritance | motion or image structure transfers from one life to another | one layer’s cadence continues in a new subject | glowing energy transfer |
| Branching | one continuity folds into multiple mutually exclusive readings | shared origin stays fixed while endpoints diverge | multiverse portal tunnel |

No scene may invent an eighth behavioral verb without recording why one of these seven cannot carry the meaning.

### Topology arc

The homepage is one continuous world changing topology, not a journey through places:

| Chapter | Topology | Depth | Composition | Visitor understanding |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Aperture | nearly flat graphite field cut by one vertical seam | `0–0.6 m` | severe asymmetry; off-canvas title | there is one line of continuity |
| Spark | suspended temporal film appears behind the seam | `0.6–3 m` | one human-scale subject against emptiness | one life changes the line |
| Convergence | independent slices synchronize | `3–9 m` | controlled compression toward one shared plane | separate lives become collective action |
| Expansion | the shared plane unfolds into contrasting scales/worlds | `4–18 m` | wide horizontal release; type becomes horizon | consequences exceed one place |
| Fracture | the registered field divides across a causal seam | `2–14 m` | opposing masses and a charged void | values can split one continuity |
| Absence | a meaningful volume is removed | depth becomes unreadable at the void | composition reorganizes around what is missing | loss alters every surviving relationship |
| Inheritance | separated fragments weave into a new directional field | `1–8 m` | diagonals resolve upward without triumphalism | legacy is behavior carried forward |
| Branch / Choice | one line becomes a restrained set of navigable possibilities | flattens back to `0–2 m` | calm map with strong editorial clarity | uncertainty can still support a choice |

### Route transformation map

Routes are different reading modes of the same continuity matter:

| Route | Mode | Dominant composition | Signature behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Guided continuum | full cinematic topology arc | scroll transforms the world; selected chapters permit Causal Shear |
| `/characters` | Lives | vertical time-slice field with human silhouette scale | focus locks one life while others shift in relative time |
| `/characters/[slug]` | One life | longitudinal editorial portrait built from five exact life movements | choices create registered before/after strata |
| `/films` | Events | full-width typographic/media index with unequal scale | focus bends one media plane forward without carousel behavior |
| `/films/[slug]` | One consequence | three-state spatial essay | Causal Shear reveals exact before/choice/after links |
| `/timeline` | Time | one continuity ribbon that folds between release and chronology | view change physically reorders one ribbon without teleporting items |
| `/watch` | Choice | calm sequence composition | paths reweave around intent, spoiler tolerance, and available time |
| `/search` | Retrieval | nearly flat, text-first field | results register instantly along the current seam |
| `/sources` | Evidence | bright, quiet editorial plane | claims and sources align without theatrical effects |
| `/about` | Method | restrained manifesto and making-of | reveals rules, rights, accessibility, and performance without a marketing hero |

## 5F. Cinematography, motion, and sound bible

### Camera grammar

- Default human lens: `55–85 mm` equivalent, used for emotional proximity and compressed layers.
- Expansion lens: `28–40 mm`, used only when world scale must visibly change.
- Detail lens: `85–120 mm`.
- Abstract strata still obey a coherent focal length, near/far plane, and parallax relationship.
- No handheld shake.
- No free orbit.
- No constant push-in.
- No camera roll except `≤ 1.2°` during Fracture and only if correspondence remains readable.
- No dolly through solid objects.
- Pointer response affects local story matter, never the main camera.
- A chapter begins with a decisive frame, changes camera state at most twice, and settles for reading.
- At least three chapters use a locked camera for `4–8 s` while internal narrative matter changes.
- Stillness is a directed shot, not missing animation.

### Signature motion vocabulary

| Narrative motion | Preparation | Travel | Resolve | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Acknowledge | `0 ms` | `70–120 ms` | `80–140 ms` | control hover/focus/press |
| Register | `60–100 ms` | `240–420 ms` | `120–180 ms` | align related temporal layers |
| Reveal | `100–180 ms` | `520–900 ms` | `700–1,400 ms` hold | expose image/type through an authored edge |
| Converge | `160–240 ms` | `760–1,300 ms` | `900–1,800 ms` hold | separate lives/events become one state |
| Shear | `120–180 ms` pressure | continuous direct mapping | `420–720 ms` reweave | inspect causality |
| Remove | `180–300 ms` recognition | `900–1,500 ms` | `1,200–2,400 ms` absence | loss |
| Transfer | existing cadence continues | `700–1,200 ms` | `800–1,500 ms` hold | inheritance |
| Fold | endpoints pin first | `650–1,100 ms` | `180–300 ms` | reorder timeline/path |

Motion rules:

- type acknowledges immediately and never imitates heavy machinery;
- visual inertia belongs to large media strata, not buttons;
- at most one dominant narrative transformation occurs at a time;
- idle motion is `0` unless it conveys time, pressure, or relationship;
- all ambient motion stops during reading and Causal Shear;
- reverse motion preserves causal registration;
- interruption uses current velocity and nearest safe state, not a snap.
- no stock `fade + translateY`;
- no spring bounce on cinematic matter;
- no uniform duration/ease across text, media, and camera;
- no transition exists solely to show WebGL capability.

### Sound grammar

Sound is optional and off by default.

Allowed original sounds:

- sparse tonal air tied to chapter tension;
- tactile, non-electronic seam pressure;
- restrained low-frequency harmonic during convergence;
- near-silence around absence;
- a transformed cadence carried into inheritance;
- quiet, immediate control acknowledgement.

Forbidden:

- copyrighted Marvel score, theme, dialogue, or recognizable effects;
- movie-trailer booms;
- alarm sirens;
- UI beeps on every hover;
- constant sci-fi drone or spaceship ambience;
- heartbeats;
- weapon sounds;
- voice assistant;
- spatial audio required to understand state.

Mix:

- default master gain `0`;
- user-enabled ambient target approximately `-30 to -24 LUFS` perceived background;
- interactions `6–10 dB` above local ambience but never startling;
- interaction harmonic fades within `280 ms` of release;
- tab hidden, reduced attention, and menu open pause nonessential audio.

## 15. Causal Shear interaction

### Interaction thesis

> The visitor pulls a visible registration seam. One apparently stable moment separates into `BEFORE`, `CHOICE`, and `AFTER`, making causal distance physically legible.

The seam is a straight or gently authored non-circular boundary already present in the composition. It never appears as a cursor follower. Hover/focus thickens it from `1 px` to `2 px` and reveals the plain-language instruction `DRAG TO SEPARATE CAUSE / EFFECT`.

### State machine

```ts
type CausalShearState =
  | "idle"
  | "discoverable"
  | "grabbed"
  | "separating"
  | "examining"
  | "reweaving"
  | "cancelled";
```

Transitions:

```text
idle → discoverable
discoverable → grabbed on pointerdown / Space
grabbed → separating after 6 px movement / Arrow input
separating → examining at a stable distance for 280 ms
examining → reweaving on release / Space / explicit Close
grabbed/separating/examining → cancelled on blur, route change, Escape, pointercancel
reweaving/cancelled → idle after all strata re-register
```

### Inputs

| Modality | Separate | Return |
|---|---|---|
| Mouse/pen | Drag seam perpendicular to its axis | Release |
| Touch | Drag visible `44 px` seam handle perpendicular to page scroll; axis lock after `8 px` | Release; vertical intent preserves page scroll |
| Keyboard | Focus seam, hold Space, use Left/Right or Up/Down arrows in `8%` steps | Release Space or press Escape |
| Reduced motion | `COMPARE BEFORE / CHOICE / AFTER` toggle shows a static registered triptych | Toggle off |
| Screen reader | Button with `aria-expanded` | Activate again |

### Direct mapping

```text
0–6 px       Acknowledge only; no content reveal
6–64 px      BEFORE and AFTER separate; no labels yet
64–160 px    CHOICE stratum becomes visible; first annotation resolves
160–240 px   causal distance uses stronger resistance; remaining annotations resolve
240 px       locked maximum; no elastic overshoot
release      annotations leave first; strata re-register with preserved velocity
```

Rules:

- Camera does not move during manipulation.
- Global scroll position does not change.
- The three strata use the same source camera/crop and stay visibly registered.
- Pull distance maps to causal separation, not shader strength.
- Resistance curve begins at `160 px`: `mapped = 160 + (raw - 160) * 0.42`.
- Each annotation sits inside the gap and points to a changed image feature; never float unrelated labels over the scene.
- Hidden content is not mounted when spoiler policy forbids it.
- Maximum three annotations.
- Release completes within `520–720 ms`, proportional to release distance.
- A tap shorter than `6 px` simply focuses the seam.
- Pointer cancel, visibility change, and route change always reset state.
- No refraction, chromatic aberration, glass distortion, scan, glow field, circular mask, particles, camera wobble, or sound boom.

## 16. Motion architecture

### Motion character

**Controlled cinematic transformation with decisive stillness.**

Rules:

1. Interface feedback is immediate and short.
2. Spatial transitions follow the seven narrative verbs and preserve visible registration.
3. Important copy receives stillness; atmosphere never competes with reading.
4. Light, image, type, and camera change together only when narrative state changes.

### Ownership matrix

| System | Sole owner | Allowed responsibility | Forbidden responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Native scroll and smoothing | Lenis instance in `ExperienceEngine` | Scroll input and current position | Directly animating DOM or camera |
| Normalized experience progress | `ExperienceEngine` | `target`, `current`, velocity, direction, active scene | React render state every frame |
| Cinematic timeline | paused GSAP timelines | Keyframed scene values from master progress | Independent scroll listeners |
| WebGL render and uniforms | React Three Fiber scene engine | Camera, materials, shaders, 3D scene | Navigation or semantic content |
| Discrete UI transitions | Motion | Menu, route, filter, modal, focus-state transitions | Scroll camera or scene scrub |
| Micro feedback | CSS | Hover, focus, press, local state | Long cinematic sequences |

Do not let GSAP ScrollTrigger, Motion, Lenis, CSS scroll timelines, and R3F independently control the same property.

### Master state

```ts
type ExperienceState = {
  target: number;
  current: number;
  previous: number;
  velocity: number;
  direction: -1 | 0 | 1;
  activeSceneId: string;
  shearTarget: number;
  shearCurrent: number;
  pointerTarget: { x: number; y: number };
  pointerCurrent: { x: number; y: number };
  quality: "static" | "low" | "medium" | "high" | "ultra";
  reducedMotion: boolean;
  reducedVisuals: boolean;
};
```

### Damping

```ts
const damp = (
  current: number,
  target: number,
  lambda: number,
  dt: number
) => current + (target - current) * (1 - Math.exp(-lambda * dt));

current = damp(current, target, 9.2, Math.min(dt, 1 / 20));
shearCurrent = damp(shearCurrent, shearTarget, shearTarget ? 11.5 : 8.0, dt);
pointerX = damp(pointerX, pointerTargetX, 13.0, dt);
pointerY = damp(pointerY, pointerTargetY, 13.0, dt);
```

Do not add more than `140 ms` apparent lag between scroll input and visual response.

### Motion tokens

```ts

    acknowledge: 0.06,
    micro: 0.16,
    quick: 0.24,
    standard: 0.42,
    panel: 0.56,

```

### Five-phase scene transition

1. **Registration** — identify the visual element that survives the transition.
2. **Pressure** — spacing, occlusion, cadence, or crop indicates that the current state can no longer hold.
3. **Transformation** — apply exactly one narrative verb to type, image matter, and depth.
4. **Resolution** — the new silhouette and copy reach canonical values.
5. **Hold** — camera and ambient motion stop long enough to understand the finding.

No scene is complete before Resolve and Hold.

### Motion limits

- Large camera travel per transition: maximum `1.4` world units.
- Camera roll: maximum `0.8°`; default `0`.
- Pointer camera offset: `0°`; pointer affects local story matter only.
- UI scale: `0.985–1.02`.
- Large typography travel: maximum `18 px`.
- Route transition: `560–820 ms`.
- Copy readable hold: minimum `1.0 s` at expected scroll pace.
- No large spring overshoot.
- No continuous idle float.
- No full-screen zoom tied 1:1 to wheel velocity.
- No moving machinery, pistons, gears, latches, partitions, rails, scanners, or greebles.

## 17. Parallax and depth choreography

### Depth thesis

Depth is a narrative variable, not permanent atmosphere. The world moves from nearly flat to deep, fractures, loses volume, and resolves flat again. Readable interface elements remain in a stable semantic plane.

### Depth bands

| Band | Role | Z relationship | Scroll factor | Pointer factor | Max desktop displacement | Mobile |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `farField` | Deep graphite/value field | `-8 to -4` | `0.04` | `0` | `10 px` | static |
| `environmentFilm` | Story-specific environment image | `-4 to -1.5` | `0.12` | `0.01` | `24 px` | `8 px` |

| `nearOccluder` | Rare type/image edge during transition | `0 to 2` | `0.42` | `0` | `56 px` | removed |
| `ui` | Semantic interface | screen space | `0` | `0` | `0` | `0` |

### Parallax rules

- Use at most four active visual bands in one stable frame.
- Near occlusion appears only during authored transitions.
- Body copy does not parallax.
- Pointer response returns to canonical state within `520 ms` after exit.
- Adjacent assets on the same plane share one transform.
- Occluders never cross navigation or body copy.
- No depth movement behind long-form reading sections.
- Reduced motion disables camera translation, pointer parallax, and velocity effects.

### Mobile translation

- Maximum three visual bands.
- No foreground interruption.
- No hover response.
- Camera travel is reduced `55–70%`, not uniformly scaled.
- The focal crop is authored separately.
- Optional device orientation is excluded from version one.

### Lighting

- Maximum two dynamic lights per active scene.

- Flat-value readability must survive when causal accents and post-processing are disabled.
- Bake story-specific environment response where possible.

### Causal Shear render path

1. Render `BEFORE`, `CHOICE`, and `AFTER` from the same camera and crop into registered targets or layered geometry.
2. Use one authored seam geometry as the manipulation boundary.
3. Map pointer/keyboard distance to layer separation and constrained depth offsets.
4. Clip each layer at the seam so correspondence stays exact.
5. Apply causal red only to the selected boundary.
6. Render semantic HTML annotations within the resulting gap.
7. Re-register all targets before releasing render resources.

Required shader uniforms:

```glsl
uniform sampler2D uBefore;
uniform sampler2D uChoice;
uniform sampler2D uAfter;
uniform vec2 uResolution;
uniform float uShearDistance;
uniform float uResistance;
uniform float uSeamPosition;
uniform float uVelocity;
uniform float uEdgeWidth;
uniform float uQuality;
```

Limits:

- Edge emission width: `≤ 1.5 px` at 1440.
- Registered targets: half resolution on medium/low; full only high/ultra.
- No full-screen distortion.
- No chromatic aberration or refraction.
- No body text rendered through the shear pass.

### Fallback

If WebGL is unavailable:

- use authored aligned `BEFORE`, `CHOICE`, and `AFTER` plates;
- use CSS clip-path/transform on capable static tiers;
- preserve the explicit compare toggle and static triptych;
- preserve every annotation;
- preserve the seam as CSS/SVG;
- preserve navigation and content;
- never show a blank dark field.

## 40. Animation ownership implementation

### Engine behavior

Use one `ExperienceEngine` object.

```ts
class ExperienceEngine {
  target = 0;
  current = 0;
  previous = 0;
  velocity = 0;
  direction: -1 | 0 | 1 = 0;
  shearTarget = 0;
  shearCurrent = 0;
  pointerTarget = { x: 0, y: 0 };
  pointerCurrent = { x: 0, y: 0 };

  tick(timeMs: number, dt: number) {
    this.lenis?.raf(timeMs);

    this.current = damp(this.current, this.target, 9.2, dt);
    this.velocity = damp(
      this.velocity,
      (this.current - this.previous) / Math.max(dt, 1 / 120),
      11,
      dt
    );
    this.direction =
      this.velocity > 0.0001 ? 1 :
      this.velocity < -0.0001 ? -1 : 0;

    this.shearCurrent = damp(
      this.shearCurrent,
      this.shearTarget,
      this.shearTarget ? 11.5 : 8,
      dt
    );

    this.pointerCurrent.x = damp(
      this.pointerCurrent.x,
      this.pointerTarget.x,
      13,
      dt
    );
    this.pointerCurrent.y = damp(
      this.pointerCurrent.y,
      this.pointerTarget.y,
      13,
      dt
    );

    this.timeline.progress(this.current);
    this.previous = this.current;
  }
}
```

Implementation rules:

- Lenis uses `autoRaf: false`.
- R3F’s active experience frame calls `engine.tick`.
- GSAP timelines are paused and receive progress from the engine.
- Do not create a ScrollTrigger for each element.
- CSS sticky sections establish scroll ranges.
- Scroll measurement updates `target` only.
- Rapid frame values stay outside React render state.
- React receives active scene changes only after the scene crosses a stable threshold.
- Route unmount destroys Lenis, GSAP timelines, listeners, render targets, and engine subscriptions.

### Lenis settings

```ts
const lenis = new Lenis({
  autoRaf: false,
  smoothWheel: true,
  syncTouch: false,
  lerp: 0.1,
  wheelMultiplier: 0.92,
  touchMultiplier: 1,
  infinite: false,
  anchors: true,
});
```

Disable Lenis for:

- reduced motion;
- static quality;
- long-form content routes when smoothing harms native behavior;
- nested focusable timelines while actively dragged.

- focused list item;

## 43. Shader and asset implementation rules

### Shaders

- Store GLSL in dedicated `.glsl` modules or typed shader strings.
- Name every uniform.
- Explain the visual purpose and safe range of every uniform.
- Clamp inputs.
- Use precision deliberately.
- Avoid dynamic loops with device-dependent cost.
- Compile one fallback material.
- Log shader compile failure in development and switch to fallback in production.
- Do not paste unexplained shader snippets from the internet.

### Geometry and topology assets

Build only geometry required to change story topology:

```text
public/assets/topology/
  temporal-film-plane.glb
  fold-ribbon.glb
  shear-seam.glb
  absence-volume.glb
  branch-ribbon.glb
  topology-manifest.json
```

- Geometry uses meters and one consistent origin/grid.
- Moving parts have named pivots, authored limits, and deterministic resting states.
- Every mesh name states material and function; do not ship `Cube.001`.
- Reuse story-matter geometry; variation comes from topology, crop, image source, depth, light, and editorial composition.
- Use Meshopt or Draco only after measuring decode and size.
- Use KTX2/Basis where visual quality survives.
- Merge static geometry where it reduces draw calls without harming culling.
- Share materials.
- Bake image-motivated response separately from causal accents.
- Use `three-mesh-bvh` only on measured raycast/culling hotspots.
- Generate LODs.
- Strip unused nodes, animation tracks, cameras, and materials.
- Verify scale, axes, pivot, bounding box, and normal/tangent quality.

### Raster

- Keep masters outside the shipped bundle.
- Runtime: AVIF primary, WebP fallback where needed.
- Preserve alpha only when required.
- Provide width/height.
- Provide responsive `srcset`.
- Do not load desktop images on mobile.
- Hash immutable files.

- global progress;

- cause progress;
- pointer normalized coordinates;
- quality tier;
- FPS/frame time;
- draw calls;

- texture memory estimate;

- active lights;

- route mode;
- active interaction and state;
- shear distance/resistance;

```ts
type QualityTier = "static" | "low" | "medium" | "high" | "ultra";
```

| Tier | DPR | Mode | Topology | Interaction | Strata target | Particles | Shadows | Effects |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

### Initial-tier signals

- viewport;
- DPR;
- hardware concurrency;
- device memory if available;
- data saver;
- reduced motion;
- reduced visuals;
- prior stable session tier.

Signals choose a starting tier only.

### Runtime governor

- Smooth frame time over at least `120` frames.
- If smoothed frame time exceeds `24 ms` for `2.5 s`, reduce one dimension.
- Cooldown `8 s`.
- Do not change tier during Causal Shear, route transition, or Absence.
- Raise only after `12 s` of stable idle performance.
- Never oscillate more than once in `20 s`.
- Keep the silhouette, dominant mass, value/light event, focal subject, continuity registration, timing, copy, and causal meaning at every tier.

Degradation order:

1. remove nonessential near occlusion;
2. reduce temporal-film subdivisions;
3. simplify fold/branch geometry while keeping silhouette;
4. halve registered-strata targets;
5. replace dynamic shadow with baked/contact approximation;
6. reduce secondary topology motion to state cuts;
